AetherPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 July 2026.

AetherPlayer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Media you open (local files, or URLs you enter) is processed on your device to play it. When you play a remote URL, your device connects directly to that server; AetherPlayer does not proxy, log, or forward that information anywhere.

The app requests network access only to play media: a local loopback used by the playback engine, and any remote URL you choose to open.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete.

Questions or issues: github.com/superuser404notfound/AetherPlayer
