AetherPlayer

A native macOS media player that plays almost anything.

Download for macOS

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.

Plays almost anything

HEVC, H.264, AV1, and VP9 with hardware and software decoding, powered by AetherEngine.

Dolby Vision and HDR

Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HDR10+ tone-mapped correctly to your display.

Disc images

Open ISO and Blu-ray folders, then pick the title and chapter you want.

Full track control

Switch audio and subtitle tracks, or load an external subtitle sidecar.

Local files or URLs

Open from Finder, drop a file, use recents, or paste a remote stream URL.

Stays current

Signed automatic updates, so you always have the latest engine fixes.
